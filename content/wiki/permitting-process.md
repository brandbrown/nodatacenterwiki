## Two Phoenix permitting systems

Phoenix runs two systems, and it matters which one holds this case:

- **Legacy PDDOnline / KIVA** (`apps-secure.phoenix.gov/PDD`) - anything accepted **before
  April 27, 2026** processes here to completion. **Case 23-1632 (filed 2023) lives here.**
  This is the primary system to monitor.
- **SHAPE PHX** (`shapephx.phoenix.gov`) - the newer Salesforce-based portal for new
  applications. You can look up status by permit number or address without an account.

## The checkpoint chain

See the live [timeline](/) for current status of each. In order:

1. Zoning / entitlement (done - legacy PUD analogous use)
2. Preliminary site plan approval (done - 2024, basis of ordinance exemption)
3. Easement abandonments (done - Council approved 2025)
4. **Final Plat** (denied - required before Certificate of Occupancy)
5. Final site plan (in correction)
6. Grading / civil / site permits (partially issued; site work active)
7. Building permits x2 (in correction)
8. SRP substation approval (pending)
9. **Air quality permits for diesel generators** (pending - public comment leverage)
10. Noise / acoustic compliance (no study in record)
11. Water / stormwater / fire & hazmat review (pending)
12. Final inspections / Certificate of Occupancy (pending - blocked by Final Plat)

## Where residents can actually intervene

- **Public comment windows** on air quality permits (Maricopa County / ADEQ).
- **Public records requests** on case 23-1632 to obtain each review-comment cycle.
- **City Council** items touching the project (District 6 covers Ahwatukee).
- **Documented compliance complaints** during active grading (stormwater, dust, noise).

> Note: Arizona has no state-level environmental review statute equivalent to California's
> CEQA, and there is likely no federal NEPA nexus here. "Environmental review" leverage runs
> primarily through **air quality permitting**, not an Environmental Impact Statement.
