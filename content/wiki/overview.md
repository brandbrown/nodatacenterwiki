## What is being built

**Menlo Digital** (through **Thistle Income Partners LLC** / Menlo Equities, a California
real estate firm) is building **MD-PHX1**, a hyperscale data center campus at
**4801-4811 E Thistle Landing Dr, Phoenix, AZ 85044**, in Ahwatukee. City case number
**23-1632**.

- **5 buildings**, roughly **1,022,400 sq ft** total
- **257 MW** of secured power (180 MW critical IT load), from a **dedicated on-site SRP substation**
- **100+ diesel backup generators**
- Located on ~38 acres, ~500 feet from a daycare and surrounded by homes, schools, and medical offices

The developer's own site targets substation and building shells in **2026 Q4** and data
hall delivery in **2027 Q1**.

## Why the zoning fight is largely over

The parcel has been zoned Commerce Park / General Commerce Park for decades. The city
treated a "Data Center Campus" as an **analogous use** under that existing zoning, approved
through an informal Zoning Administrator interpretation rather than a public rezoning. The
project then received **preliminary site plan approval in 2024**.

That 2024 approval matters enormously: when Phoenix adopted a stricter data center ordinance
in **2025** (new setbacks and review requirements), the City Council set an applicability
cutoff and determined the new rules **do not apply** to projects that already had preliminary
site approval. MD-PHX1 was grandfathered out.

## Where residents can still be heard

This is a **permitting and compliance** fight, not a zoning fight. As of the community's
latest public accounting:

- The **Final Plat is denied** by the city's own reviewers (it is required before any
  Certificate of Occupancy).
- The **final site plan, substation, and both building permits** remain in active correction.
- There was **no noise study, water analysis, or environmental review** in the public record.

The highest-value, legally recognized opening is the **air quality permitting** for 100+
diesel generators through the **Maricopa County Air Quality Department** and/or **ADEQ**,
which carry **public comment periods**. See the [Permitting Process](../permitting-process/)
and [Air Quality & Diesel Generators](../air-quality-diesel-generators/) pages.
