## What this is

An automated, git-backed watchdog for the MD-PHX1 data center (City case 23-1632). It:

- Maintains a **live timeline** of the project's zoning and permitting checkpoints.
- **Scans official and news sources daily** for new documents, filings, and status changes.
- Runs new documents through **domain lenses** (acoustic, air/EPA, water, fire, legal) to
  surface compliance concerns and delay opportunities.
- Publishes a **Latest Developments** feed and sends an **internal digest** to organizers
  when something actually changes.

## What it monitors

- City of Phoenix PDDOnline (legacy) case 23-1632 and SHAPE PHX status
- Menlo Digital's own development-status page
- Maricopa County Air Quality and ADEQ public notices
- Phoenix City Council agendas and minutes
- Local news (ahwatukee.com) and the Alliance site

## Guardrails

- The **public site publishes only factual, source-linked information**.
- **Strategic and legal leads are kept internal** and reviewed by a human (and the Alliance's
  attorney) before any wider distribution. Nothing here is legal advice.
- All AI-generated analysis is labeled as such and treated as a draft pending human review.
