## Actions that matter most

1. **Watch for the air quality permit comment window.** This is the highest-value action.
   When the Maricopa County / ADEQ public comment period opens, submit substantive comments.
   The watchdog will flag it the day it appears.
2. **File and share public records requests** on **City of Phoenix Project 23-1632**. Every
   review-comment cycle is public and documents deficiencies.
3. **Contact your City Council member.** District 6 covers Ahwatukee - Councilman Kevin
   Robinson: (602) 262-7485, council.district.6@phoenix.gov. Ask for a full public process.
4. **Attend Alliance meetings** (roughly every other weekend) and community hearings.
5. **Document on-site compliance issues** during grading - dust, stormwater, noise - with
   dates, photos, and times.

## Get involved with the Alliance

The **Ahwatukee Community Alliance Inc.** (a 501(c)(3)) coordinates legal counsel, acoustic
and environmental studies, and representation in the city's process. Join the email list and
support the effort at [nodatacenterahwatukee.org](https://nodatacenterahwatukee.org/).

> This site is a volunteer-built watchdog tool. It publishes only factual, source-linked
> information. Legal strategy is directed by the Alliance and its attorney.
